# Code of Conduct

TC Pioneer is committed to providing a respectful and constructive community for learning, research, and collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional
- Welcome people with different experience levels
- Give constructive feedback
- Respect privacy and personal boundaries
- Keep security research ethical and authorized

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, targeted abuse, doxxing, malicious activity, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior violates this code of conduct.

For concerns, contact the project maintainers privately rather than escalating conflicts publicly.
